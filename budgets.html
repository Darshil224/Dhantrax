Budget Status Guide

Budget status is calculated from the percentage of your budget that has been spent. As spending increases, the budget card changes to help you identify categories that need attention.
No Badge (Normal)

Below 80% of the budget has been spent. Cards without a badge are in the normal range.

Near Limit

80% to 99% of the budget has been spent.

Budget Reached

100% of the budget has been used.

Over Budget

Spending has exceeded the allocated budget.
